Strategy 2: The "Bounce Back" Support & Resistance Strategy
This strategy is based on the idea of trading reversals from significant price levels, with confirmation from price action and an oscillator.

Core Principle: Identify key support and resistance levels and look for signs of a reversal at those levels.

Indicators for Confluence:

Key Levels: Fibonacci Retracement levels. These are drawn on major price swings to identify potential turning points. The 50% and 61.8% levels are often the most powerful.

Price Action Confirmation: A specific candlestick pattern, such as a Bullish Engulfing pattern at a support level, or a Bearish Engulfing or Pin Bar at a resistance level.

Momentum Confirmation: Relative Strength Index (RSI) Divergence. For a potential buy signal at a support level, you would look for the price to be making a lower low while the RSI is making a higher low (bullish divergence).

EA Logic for Automation:

Buy Signal: Price is at a key Fibonacci support level AND a Bullish Engulfing candle forms AND bullish RSI divergence is present.

Sell Signal: Price is at a key Fibonacci resistance level AND a Bearish Engulfing candle forms AND bearish RSI divergence is present.

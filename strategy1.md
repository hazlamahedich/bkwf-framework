Strategy 1: The "Triple Threat" Trend-Following Strategy
This is a classic and powerful approach that combines trend, momentum, and a dynamic entry signal. It's well-suited for automation because its rules are clear and quantitative.

Core Principle: Only take trades in the direction of the long-term trend, and time your entries with a short-term momentum shift.

Indicators for Confluence:

Long-Term Trend: 200-period Exponential Moving Average (EMA). If the price is above the 200 EMA, the primary trend is up. If below, the trend is down.

Medium-Term Momentum: Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD). The MACD must be in agreement with the long-term trend. For an uptrend, the MACD line should be above its signal line. For a downtrend, the MACD should be below its signal line.

Short-Term Entry: Stochastic Oscillator. This is used to pinpoint entry points. For a long trade, you'd look for the Stochastic to be in the oversold area (below 20) and crossing up. For a short trade, you'd look for the Stochastic to be in the overbought area (above 80) and crossing down.

EA Logic for Automation:

Buy Signal: Price > 200 EMA AND MACD Line > MACD Signal Line AND Stochastic crosses up from below 20.

Sell Signal: Price < 200 EMA AND MACD Line < MACD Signal Line AND Stochastic crosses down from above 80.

- **Best Times:** Evenings (7-9 PM) and weekends when people are relaxed
- **Frequency:** Test different versions 2-3 days apart
- **Day of Week:** Tuesday, Wednesday, and Saturday perform best

### **Engagement Tactics**
1. **Ask for specific reactions** (🙋‍♀️, 💪, 😴) 
2. **Use relatable scenarios** (marriage problems, teenager stories)
3. **Create conversation starters** ("Anyone else feel this way?")
4. **Share personal vulnerabilities** (admitting mistakes, learning experiences)

- Before/after screenshots of portfolio performance
- Simple infographics comparing manual vs. automated trading
- Behind-the-scenes videos of you explaining the system

1. **Respond to every comment within 2 hours**
